Concert poster process

Age of Romance

A visual progression from hand-drawn concept to a romantic Y2K concert poster, combining classical ruins, soft sunset skies, and liquid-silver ornament.

✦ · ◇ · ✧ · ☾ · ✦
✦ ◇ ✧ ✦
01
The First Sketch
Concept

Create a realistic poster for Silence Wang’s Age of Romance concert using my sketch. Use silver and pink Y2K typography with the title centered at the top and metallic Y2K elements around the title and lower-left corner. Create a background of Greek and Roman-inspired ruins surrounded by vines, flowers, and bushes. The upper 35% should feature a pink-and-blue sunset with Y2K-style clouds.

◇ ✦ ✦ ✧
02
Draft 1
Exploration

Make all Y2K elements silver and metallic, removing the pink accents. Shift the sky to a cooler, more grey-toned atmosphere.

✧ ◇ ✦ ◇
03
Draft 2
Refinement

Keep the ruins, typography, and Y2K elements from this version, but replace only the sky with the sky from the previous version.

✦ ✧ ◇ ✦
04
The Final Poster
Final

Keep the ruins, typography, and Y2K elements from this version, but replace only the sky with the sky from the previous version. Use the star from the first version and make it silver only.
