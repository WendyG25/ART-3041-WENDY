The city, distilled.

A city of a thousand scents

New York is made of moments you wish you could keep.

Four neighborhoods / Four fragrant portraits

Magnolia blossoms soften Brooklyn brownstones. In Greenwich Village, café doors open to the first green light of morning. Along the Hudson River, cedar and leaves drift on the breeze. On Fifth Avenue, rain turns the pavement into a shimmering reflection of the city.

Four fragrances, each capturing a place, a feeling, and a fleeting New York moment to make your own.

Discover Brooklyn Bloom
01 / THE SCENT IN FOUR FORMS

Brooklyn Bloom

In Brooklyn Heights, spring reveals itself in the smallest details: magnolia blossoms over iron railings, peonies brightening brownstone stoops, and warm sunlight reaching the quiet, tree lined streets. Their soft floral notes mingle with white musk, recalling a leisurely walk past garden gates and familiar doorways. Brooklyn Bloom captures the gentle romance of a neighborhood coming into flower.

Greenwich Morning

Greenwich Village stirs to life with the clink of café cups, open windows, and golden light spilling across its winding streets. Bright bergamot brings a spark of energy, green tea lends a delicate calm, and fresh basil evokes leafy courtyards tucked between historic brick buildings. Greenwich Morning holds the feeling of those early hours when the city feels entirely your own.

Riverside

03 / THE SCENT IN FOUR FORMS

Riverside

Beside the Hudson River, the rhythm of New York begins to soften. A breeze travels through the trees along the waterfront, past weathered piers and benches facing the distant Manhattan skyline. Cedarwood and vetiver echo the warmth of wood and earth, while green leaves lend the freshness of open air. Riverside preserves that rare, grounding moment when the city seems to exhale.

Fifth Avenue Rain

IRIS · VIOLET LEAF · WHITE MUSK · RAIN

Discover Fifth Avenue Rain
04 / THE SCENT IN FOUR FORMS

Fifth Avenue Rain

After a spring shower, Fifth Avenue becomes a tapestry of reflections, with the Flatiron Building and surrounding stone façades shimmering above the wet pavement. The elegant softness of iris meets crisp violet leaf and airy white musk, while a delicate rain accord recalls freshly washed streets. Fifth Avenue Rain captures the quiet clarity that follows a storm, when familiar corners of Manhattan feel unexpectedly new.
