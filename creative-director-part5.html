01   /   THE CAMPAIGN CONCEPT

A City of a Thousand Scents.

New York is a city of countless stories, each shaped by its atmosphere, architecture, and memories. Les Essences de New York translates fleeting moments into four fragrances, celebrating the intimate beauty hidden in everyday life.

02   /   THE DESIGN LANGUAGE

Botanical poetry meets New York.

Inspired by the artistic heritage of French perfumery, the visual identity combines botanical engravings, refined serif typography, and distinctive New York architecture. Each scent has its own palette while remaining part of one unified collection.
