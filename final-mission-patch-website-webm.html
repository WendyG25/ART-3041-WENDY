Artist Statement

Design concept · Symbols · Scientific discovery · AI disclosure

My final mission patch represents Baruch College’s involvement in space research and its commitment to learning, exploration, and scientific discovery. I wanted the design to feel modern and recognizable, while using simple imagery to communicate the connection between the college and space. The overall composition is inspired by traditional mission patches, but uses a clean, minimal style.

The large circular outline in the center represents a planet and the possibilities of exploring beyond Earth. A diagonal orbital ring passes through the circle to suggest movement, progress, and the continuous process of discovery. The two stars represent curiosity and new discoveries, while the small dot suggests a distant celestial object. I chose a hexagonal patch shape because its geometric structure creates a sense of stability and precision. The double white border gives the design a finished appearance and emphasizes its connection to scientific and technical work.

The light blue background and white elements create a limited color palette that feels calm, futuristic, and easy to recognize. I placed “BARUCH” in large, bold letters across the center to make the college the main focus, while the smaller “SPACE RESEARCH” text identifies the mission. Together, these elements communicate how Baruch can contribute to research and discovery in space.

I used AI tools, including ChatGPT, to help develop and refine the mission patch generator, experiment with layouts, and implement design and animation features. I selected and refined the final symbols, typography, colors, and composition.
