A Style Guide for Your AI

My visual rules for a class homepage that feels dreamy, editorial, and distinctly mine. These are the decisions I want AI to carry through every revision.

The Page

Wendy Gao
Work
Structure & inspiration

Build a single-page class homepage: a full-width hero image first, my name inside its top-right corner, and the assignment grid immediately below. Keep all navigation and content on that page.

Draw on Flower Knows for romantic luxury, delicate ornament, soft gradients, frames, and editorial beauty styling. Draw on Cider for clean structure, spacing, confident hierarchy, and subtle link emphasis. Do not copy either site.

The hero is 45–55vh tall (360–650px), uses object-fit: cover, and may have a soft overlay. Position my name 32px from the top and right on desktop, 20px on mobile.

Soft pastel luxury

My general palette combines blush white and pearl white with soft blush, pastel pink, lavender, gentle blue, and subtle gold. Keep the overall feeling airy, romantic, and softly layered.

Use blush white and pearl for backgrounds and surfaces, blush and pale blue for soft sections, pink and lavender for accents, and #E8D6A7 for delicate gold decorations. Choose readable text tones that harmonize with the palette. Avoid dark themes, neon, harsh saturation, and heavy shadows.

03

The Typography

Dream in detail.Space, softness, and a considered point of view.✦ · ✿ · ✧
An airy editorial rhythm

Use Daith Big Medium Italic for every heading at Medium weight and DM Sans Light for body copy at Light weight. Add no other display font. Let type, spacing, and hierarchy do most of the work.

H1: 56–80px; H2: 36–56px; H3: 24–32px; labels: 14px; body: 16–18px with 1.6 line-height. Heading line-height is 1.15. Preserve generous whitespace: 56–80px between sections, 16–24px between cards, and refined 1.5–2px borders in #E6D8F3.

Center content at a maximum of 1200px. Use two equal columns with a 32px gap on laptops. On phones, keep two columns when each can be at least 140px wide, with a 12–16px gap. Use 48px desktop, 32px tablet, and 16px mobile side padding; breakpoints are 1024px+, 768–1023px, and 320–767px.

The Details

Assignment link ✦Another assignment ✧
Interaction & atmosphere

Assignment links are full-column unfilled buttons with accent text and borders, no default shadow, 14px rounded square corners, at least 56px height, and 16px vertical × 24px horizontal padding. On hover, underline and intensify the active link, while gently fading the rest. Transitions last 250–350ms.

Use #E8D6A7 for floral ornaments, corner stars, and delicate gold details. Keep the glitter small, lavender, soft gold, and pearl-white, behind the content, and at no more than 0.25 opacity. Layer slow continuous movement; no bouncing, spinning, or aggressive animation. Use ornaments sparingly so cards feel lightly stacked and the content stays clear.

Give every interactive element a visible focus state, keep links recognizable without hover, ensure text remains legible, and respect reduced-motion settings.
